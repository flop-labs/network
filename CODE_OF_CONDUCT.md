# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: argue with the idea, not the person. Critique of the protocol, its economics, or our
decisions is welcome here, including sharp critique. Harassment, personal attacks, and spam are not.

Report conduct problems privately to `security@flop.finance`. Reports are read by FLOP Labs
maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions, and block
accounts, that break this code.
