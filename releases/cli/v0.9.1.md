### Changed

- Takes `@flop-labs/sdk` 0.12.1, which admits runtime `1014039`, so chain commands keep working on
  Devnet and Testnet after their upgrade to `1014039`; 0.9.0 refuses that spec with
  `RUNTIME_INCOMPATIBLE`.

### Fixed

- `model onboard --wait-capture` sends the `INFERENCE_API_KEY` bearer when it polls the capture
  endpoint's `/health`. The TOPLOC backend answers 401 without it, so the wait ran to its deadline
  and failed against every authenticated backend.
