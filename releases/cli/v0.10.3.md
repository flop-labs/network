### Fixed

- `flop validator join` reaches `ready_for_promotion` once its `session.setKeys` finalizes. Before,
  every run after the call landed stopped in `retry_pending` and recreated the node. The receipt
  scan compared the block header number with a method a `Compact<BlockNumber>` lacks, and the
  resulting `TypeError` was retried as a temporary failure. A `retry_pending` receipt in
  `.join-status.json` now also carries `failedStep` (runner stage and join step, such as
  `registration/reconcile_setKeys`) and `errorClass` (such as `TypeError`), never the error text.
  `JOIN.md` documents both fields.
