### Added

- Add resumable `flop validator join` for approved Devnet/Testnet releases, with protected
  local account import, persistent funding wait and finalized transaction recovery.

### Changed

- `flop model onboard` registers the pinned DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 checkpoint: it still binds
  the pinned revision and content root before any weight transfer (and binds a local source's
  measured root before any chain write), then takes `params_billion` from the stored-tensor census
  (763,205,315,794 parameters, so 763B); `--params-billion` must equal it. The release workflow no
  longer reports a census blocker for the pinned checkpoint.

### Fixed

- `flop launch --pay wallet|agent-key` on devnet reads miner-signed offers from
  `https://explorer.devnet.flop.network` (or `$FLOP_INDEXER_URL`) when neither `--indexer` nor
  `--enclave-key` is given, and without `--window-hours` takes offers of 20 minutes or more. With
  the default flags it previously saw no offers, or none long enough for its 1-hour window, and
  fell back to a bare open whose escrow exceeded `--max-channel-flop`.
