### Fixed

- `flop validator join` recovers when another signer on the validator account takes the nonce of
  its journaled `session.setKeys` or `validators.register`. The bundle's DA service announces its
  endpoint from that account and did this on Testnet, which left a sponsored, registered account
  without session keys and stopped the join as `blocked`. Once the finalized nonce has passed the
  journaled one and the exact signed bytes are absent from every finalized block of their era,
  the runner records them under `superseded` in `.validator-join.json` and signs the same call at
  the live nonce. A join an older CLI already blocked resumes after its `.join-status.json` is
  moved aside, as `JOIN.md` describes.
