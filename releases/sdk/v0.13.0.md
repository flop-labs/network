### Changed

- `runDirectSession` force-opens channels with a 1200-second lifetime by default, up from 120 s.
  The chain requires settle within `max_duration` blocks of the acknowledgement, and a worker's
  close (DA publication of the TOPLOC activations and transcript, evidence, then settle) took
  about 4.5 minutes on Testnet, so sessions failed with `AttestationStale`. 1200 s matches the
  standing offers (#4565) and Hermes direct (#3870).
- `maxDurationSeconds` is accepted with `via: "auto"` and `via: "offer"` as well as `"force"`.
  Given explicitly, it sets the force-open lifetime and a signed offer is taken only when it grants
  at least that many blocks.

### Fixed

- `runDirectSession` refuses a `force_open`, whether requested with `via: "force"` or reached as the
  `auto` fallback, when the named miner has no unexpired accepted calibration at the finalized head.
  The chain's `force_ack` needs that calibration, so such a channel could only expire. Before this,
  the escrow sat in `PendingAck` for the whole ack window before the refund. No escrow is submitted and
  `onPrepared` is not called. `DirectSessionRuntime` gains `minerCalibrated()`.
