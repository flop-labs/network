### Changed

- `SUPPORTED_SPEC_VERSIONS` admits devnet runtimes `1014037` (candidates 1.14.36-1.14.37: the
  `CalibrationOracles` membership pallet, #4731, and `ProxyType::SudoOperational`, #4732) and
  `1014038` (candidate 1.14.38: `impl_name` `flop-node`, #4764), and
  `SUPPORTED_SPEC_VERSION_RANGE` now ends at `1014038`. `1014034` and `1014035` stay admitted so
  clients work before and after `setCode` and on the public Testnet; `1014036` was never built
  from main and stays rejected.

### Added

- `flop_sdk.NETWORK_PROFILES` and `get_network_profile`, mirroring the TypeScript SDK's profiles.
  Testnet is live at `wss://testnet.flop.network` and pinned to genesis `0x918abd58…cfd800`. Its
  `gateway` is `https://gateway.testnet.flop.network` (#4720), which is not live yet; the SDK
  only exposes the value and does not dial it.
- `FlopClientOptions.for_network(name)` and `SubstrateOptions.for_network(name, seed)` build
  options from a profile, including its genesis pin.
- `expected_genesis_hash` on `FlopClientOptions` and `SubstrateOptions`. When it is set, `connect()`
  and `SubstrateClient()` read the genesis block hash and refuse any other chain with
  `FLOPError(code="GENESIS_MISMATCH")`. A malformed pin raises `ValueError`. Without a pin, nothing
  changes.
